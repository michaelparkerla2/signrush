# Game home and repeated pilot tasks

The Home screen displays private server-derived counts of available signing/review tasks and the player's submitted, pending and approved totals. Current tasks can be resumed. Closed signing jobs and saved review jobs can be replaced with a new request, while immutable recording/review records retain the prior work. Consensus only updates a current job when its recording/review ID still matches; historical rewards remain idempotent.

The existing global limit of three recording reservations, 8 MiB per upload and five reviews per recording is unchanged. Availability is a snapshot refreshed roughly every ten seconds, not a reservation guarantee. Prompts and other players' answers never enter dashboard documents. The pilot backend remains a manually started, maximum-30-minute Cloud Shell worker.

Visual direction: warm coral, mint, blue task cards and violet rewards. Every 25 actual test points marks a star milestone. Encouragement after submission acknowledges the effort, not translation accuracy. Earned-point celebrations trigger only on an observed increase, with reduced-motion support. No fabricated balance, streak, countdown, scarcity or payout promise is shown.

Cash rewards remain a clearly labeled future feature. Before outside invitations, complete branding and phone usability review, calibrated scoring/adjudication, durable hosting, fraud controls and a real payout integration. The intended cash program needs a funded, separate cash ledger, settled earnings, a $50 eligible payout minimum, supported payment-provider onboarding, withdrawal requests, duplicate-payment protection, payout status/history and failure recovery. PayPal/Venmo availability and requirements must be verified at integration time. Do not collect payment credentials in the current pilot. Test points are never retroactively represented as cash.

## Cash wallet and sharing update

Cash out opens a modal wallet with live test points, zero pilot cash balance, a $50 threshold and inactive withdrawal action. The owner confirmed the future conversion is one approved cash point per cent (5,000 = $50); pilot points remain nonredeemable. The proposed validation-plus-payout window is up to 90 days from submission, displayed in readable text before withdrawal alongside accuracy requirements. Final payment timing and integration remain unlaunched.

The owner confirmed the $5,000 bonus pool is planned and not funded. Its display therefore shows a target, $0 funding and not-open status, not a live contest or growing funded prize. High performance in both roles is a visible proposed eligibility condition. Exact ranking, minimum task counts, tie-breaks and contest dates still need definition.

Share uses the device share sheet, X/Facebook compose links or Copy link. No messages are sent automatically. The shared wording identifies the invitation-only private pilot. These links do not track referrals or grant points: a future referral system must verify a distinct new member and qualifying activity before rewards. No payment details are collected.
