Rules & rewards

Current participation and reward information · Updated September 23, 2026.

Play fair. Sign clearly.

  • Use good lighting and keep your face, shoulders, arms and hands visible.
  • Sign the assigned meaning accurately. Reviewers watch the silent video and independently write its meaning.
  • You cannot review your own submission. Do not share prompts, answers or accounts.
  • Submissions require independent review before points are approved. Unclear or conflicting results may require additional review; submitting a task does not guarantee approval.

Keep the corpus real: three independent unusable-video reports can remove a clip. Poor quality alone is not a cheating strike. Three independent spam/non-signing reports create one strike. The first two strikes revoke only points from those videos; a third forfeits all remaining test points and disables signing and validation. Full terms.

Understanding the $36/hour equivalent

UMI has committed to a cash rate of $0.01 per approved signing second: $0.10–$0.25 for an approved 10–25-second signing challenge.

$36 is a time equivalent: 3,600 continuously approved signing seconds × $0.01. It is not an hourly wage or a guarantee. Preparation, retakes, uploads, waiting and unavailable tasks reduce earnings per hour spent participating. The signing rate does not apply to time spent reviewing videos.

The current app records test points, not cash earnings. Cash tracking is being enabled. Existing test points have no cash value and do not automatically convert to dollars.

The intended cash-out minimum is $50 in approved cash earnings. Saved PayPal or Venmo details are payout preferences, not verified or connected payment accounts. Cash withdrawal is not currently available.

$5,000 Bonus Pool

UMI has committed $5,000 to a bonus competition. This is the total pool, not an individual award or a guaranteed payment to every player.

The bonus competition is not accepting entries. No current ranking, point total or task completion establishes prize eligibility. Participation eligibility, scoring, tie-breaks, dates and award allocation have not been established; there are no active bonus award terms.
